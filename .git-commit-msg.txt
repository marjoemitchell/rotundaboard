Add a link to the bill's official record on bills.legmt.gov

The bill drawer and full page only ever linked to our own rendering of
a bill (status history, votes, cosponsors) — nothing pointed at the
actual government record. Found and verified the URL scheme
bills.legmt.gov's own SPA uses to key a bill (legislature id + LC draft
number, not bill type/number), confirmed it resolves correctly for
both an enacted law and a bill that died in committee, and added a
"View on official site" link next to the identifier in both places,
matching the pattern already used on the committee detail page.
